POKEMON BATTLE LAB - REAL-TIME ROSTER / 22 OCTOBER 2026

Double-click Play Battle Lab.cmd.
9 Pokemon, 15 bodies, six move choices each; four equipped at once.

WASD: move the Pokemon
Shift: walk
Hold right mouse: orbit camera
Mouse wheel: zoom
Space: dash between attacks
1, 2, 3, 4: attacks (move closer for melee)
Tab: Setup, model source, species and move loadout
Click each move slot in Setup to cycle the six choices.
R: reset
Esc: pause
F1: model viewer

Starts with active AI and finite HP. For animation study, enable Practice and
turn off Opponent commands in Setup. Sound volume is below the four move slots.

Try each move while standing, after running, and after a dash. Check that you
can move again after recovery. For a problem, note Pokemon / model source /
move and what looked or sounded wrong. README lists every available move.

ZA and BDSP effects are imported. Arceus/SV bodies use ZA effects/timing.
Sound uses Emerald recordings with substitutes for newer moves. Native shader,
state-machine and hitbox behavior remain incomplete; secondary move effects
are not implemented. Passing tests do not establish good battle feel.

Play Animation Study.cmd preserves the previous direct-control build.
Play Original Lab.cmd preserves the initial prototype. No HoennVR saves used.
