POKEMON BATTLE LAB - DIRECT CONTROL / 22 OCTOBER 2026

Double-click Play Battle Lab.cmd.

WASD: move the Pokemon
Shift: walk
Hold right mouse: orbit camera
Mouse wheel: zoom
Space: dash between attacks
1, 2, 3: attacks (move closer for melee)
Tab: Setup, model source, species, AI and practice options
R: reset
Esc: pause
F1: three-source model viewer

Starts with a passive target and unlimited practice HP. Enable opponent commands
and turn Practice off in Setup to fight. Attacks commit movement until recovery.

ZA and BDSP effects are imported. Arceus uses its own body animations with ZA
effects/timing, clearly labeled. Native shaders/state machines remain incomplete.

Play Original Lab.cmd preserves the earlier prototype. No HoennVR saves are used.
